Welcome to the first release of PinBoard!

• Save pictures, videos, GIFs, notes, quotes and links as pins, and gather them on boards and sub-boards
• Share to PinBoard from any app and choose a board right in the share sheet
• On-device AI tags, titles and sorts your pins, and finds look-alikes before you save twice
• Search by words, colors, the text in a picture or what a video says
• A Hidden side behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode
• Photo, Photo Grid and Quote widgets for your Home Screen
• Optional iCloud sync between your iPhone and iPad, and separate profiles
• Export everything whenever you like

No account, no ads, no tracking. Thanks for trying PinBoard — ideas and bug reports are always welcome.
