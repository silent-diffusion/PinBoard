# How to use PinBoard

PinBoard is your own private inspiration library. You save things as **pins**, gather them on **boards**, and find them again by what they show or say. This guide takes about ten minutes to read; dip into any section.

---

## 1. The four tabs

- **Pins** — everything you've saved, newest first or shuffled. **Unsorted** shows pins that aren't on a board yet.
- **Boards** — your boards and sub-boards, favorites first, plus your smart boards.
- **Explore** — search openly licensed pictures on the web and save the ones you like.
- **Search** — find anything in your library.

Swipe left or right to move between tabs. **Settings** is the gear button; type in its search field to find any setting. To change PinBoard's Home Screen icon, choose one of eight looks in **Settings › Appearance › App icon**.

## 2. Saving pins

Tap **+** on the Pins tab or on a board:

- **Photos…** or **Files…** — pick pictures, videos, GIFs and text files.
- **Camera** — take a photo.
- **Clipboard** — whatever you copied: a picture, text or a link.
- **Text pin** — write a note, quote, idea or list.
- **Link pin** — paste a web address. PinBoard reads the page and fills in its title, picture and text.

On iPad you can also drag things in from Safari, Photos and other apps.

**Videos from TikTok, Instagram and similar sites** are saved as a link with their picture; PinBoard doesn't download them. To keep the video itself, save it in that app first (most have a Save or Download button), then add it from Photos or with the share sheet.

**From other apps:** tap the Share button and choose **PinBoard** (see section 9).

If something looks like a pin you already have, PinBoard asks whether to save it again, add the existing one to the board, or skip it.

## 3. Boards

- Make a board with **New board** on the Boards tab. Boards can hold **sub-boards** as deep as you like ("Travel › Japan › Kyoto").
- Add pins to a board from a pin's menu (**Add to board…**), by selecting several pins, or by dragging.
- Touch and hold a board for its menu: rename, change the cover, add to favorites, hide, merge, delete.
- **Smart boards** fill themselves: make a search you like on the Search tab and choose **Save as smart board**.
- **Sort into boards** (in the **⋯ › AI sorting** menu) suggests where your pins belong. Each pin's page shows why.

## 4. Looking at your pins

- Tap a card to open it full screen. Swipe sideways for the next one, pinch to zoom, swipe down to close.
- Open a pin's page for everything about it: title, note, tags, boards, link details and **More like this**.
- **Videos:** tap for the controls, use the skip buttons, and touch and hold for 2× speed.
- **Edit pictures** with Crop, Rotate and Flip. Your Photos library is never changed.
- **Articles** you saved as links have **Read article**. Select words to **Highlight** them or **Save as pin** as a quote.

## 5. The AI

PinBoard's AI runs on your device and works quietly in the background:

- **Tags** for what's in a picture, its colors and any words in it.
- **Titles** for pins without one. You'll see "Written by AI" and can undo it.
- **Speech in videos** is written down into the pin's note, so you can search for what was said.
- **Look-alikes** are spotted before you save a picture twice. **Settings › Library › Possible duplicates** lists the ones already in your library.

On devices with Apple Intelligence, PinBoard can use it for more natural titles and search words.

**Cloud AI (optional):** for even more specific tags, you can add your own key for Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI in **Settings › AI**. PinBoard names the company, shows exactly what is sent and asks before turning it on. You pay the provider directly, usually a fraction of a cent per picture. Pins on the Hidden side are left out of all of this (see section 8).

## 6. Finding things

Type anything on the **Search** tab: words in a title or note, a tag, a color ("red"), text that appears in a picture, or something said in a video. Narrow it down with the filters for kind, board and date. Every pin also has **More like this**.

## 7. Explore

Pick a picture source in the Explore tab's top bar (Wikimedia Commons needs nothing; Pexels, Pixabay and Unsplash need a free key you paste in **Settings › Explore & web search**). Search, then tap **+** to save a picture, or save it straight to a board. Nothing is fetched until you search.

At the end of a board, **Keep exploring** offers web pictures that fit the board.

## 8. The Hidden side

For things you'd rather keep to yourself.

- Open it from **Boards › ⋯ › Hidden…** It asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
- Hide a board by touching and holding it and choosing **Hide board**; hide a pin with **Hide pin**.
- Hidden pins never show up in your feed, search, suggestions, widgets or the share sheet, and the screen is covered when you switch apps.
- Hidden locks again when you leave PinBoard, switch profiles or choose **Lock now**.
- **No AI on hidden pins, unless you want it.** By default hidden pins get no AI tags, titles, transcripts or Cloud AI; you find them by their titles, notes and your own tags. To let the AI work on them too, turn on **Include hidden pins in AI** in Settings (type "hidden" in Settings' search to find it).

## 9. Saving from other apps (the share sheet)

1. In Photos, Safari, Files, Messages or another app, tap the **Share** button.
2. Choose **PinBoard**. (First time? Scroll to the end of the app row, tap **More**, and switch PinBoard on.)
3. Choose a **board**: suggested boards and the one you used last are at the top, and you can search or make a new board right there. With more than one profile, choose the **profile** too.
4. Add a note if you like, and tap **Save**.

PinBoard adds it the next time you open the app. If something looks familiar, the sheet tells you and lets you add the pin you already have instead.

**Quick save:** turn off **Choose a board when sharing** in **Settings › Pins & boards**, and sharing saves in one tap, to Unsorted.

## 10. Widgets

1. Touch and hold your Home Screen and tap **Edit › Add Widget**.
2. Search for **PinBoard** and choose **Photo**, **Photo Grid** or **Quote**, in any size.
3. Touch and hold the widget, choose **Edit Widget**, and pick a **Board** (or leave it empty for all boards).

Widgets change every hour. Tap one to open that pin. For two widgets on the same board, give each a different **Pick** so they show different things.

## 11. iCloud sync and storage

- Turn on **Sync with iCloud Drive** in **Settings › iCloud & storage** on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account.
- Each profile syncs on its own, and profiles pair up by name across devices.
- **Sync log** shows what each sync did. **Sync now** or a pull-down on any grid starts one right away.
- **Optimize device storage** keeps older full-size files only in iCloud and brings them back when you open them.
- **Video quality** makes stored videos smaller to save space.

## 12. Profiles

A profile is a completely separate library with its own settings, Hidden side and iCloud choice: handy for keeping work and home apart. Make, rename, switch and delete profiles in **Settings › Library › Profiles**.

## 13. Your data

- **Export all pins and boards…** (Settings › Library) makes folders, one per board, with titles, tags and notes written into each picture. Keep it somewhere safe.
- **Import a folder…** brings an export back, or turns any folder of pictures into boards.
- **Recently Deleted** keeps deleted pins for 30 days.
- **Recover library** goes back to a safety copy of your library from a few minutes ago or the day before.

## 14. Privacy in one paragraph

Your library stays on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer, ever. PinBoard goes online only when you search the web, save or open a link, or switch on iCloud sync or Cloud AI. **Offline mode** turns all of that off with one switch. The full [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) explains each case.
