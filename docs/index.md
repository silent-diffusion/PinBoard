# PinBoard

**A private place for the pictures, videos, notes and links you love.**

PinBoard is a personal inspiration library for iPhone and iPad (and Macs with Apple silicon). Save things as pins, gather them on boards, and find anything again by what it shows or says. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and the AI that tags and sorts your pins runs on your device.

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn it on.
